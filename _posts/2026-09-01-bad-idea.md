## Is a bad idea not the core problem of a process, what if you're already doing something wrong, will AI fix it?

What if you have all the money in the world, a top leading development team to built the perfect application, but the core idea itself is fundamentally flawed? Will AI be able to fix it, or will it just optimize a bad concept? Although AI can improve execution, it will make a bad idea, an expensive bad idea.

There are so many reasons why a product might fail, is there a need, are you unique, how does it fit the need, is the timing right, and can it scale effectively? 

Pros and Cons
### Pros
- Fail fast, allowing you to pivot or iterate on the idea quickly.
- If succesfull, it can lead to significant cost savings.
- Ability to fulfill a multi-disciplinary approach by leveraging AI expertise across different domains by a single founder

### Cons
- Costs, time, and resources may be wasted on a flawed concept.
- Reputation damage if the product fails publicly.
