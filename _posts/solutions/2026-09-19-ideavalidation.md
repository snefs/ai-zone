## Idea validation

As an entrepreneur, I've worked on countless prototypes, demos, hobby projects only to learn a new skill, to analyze a problem, and even creating games to entertain my kids with selfmade puzzles....it took me evenings, weekends, mornings and sometimes I couldn't stop working on this and didn't sleep, as my mind was set focused on reaching a certain validation point.

My approach has always been (for personal projects):
- New idea -> spent max 30min to validate if it is feasible
    - In case it is -> spent iterations of small tasks to work towards a prototype
    - Define an intermediate mid term goal, and long term goal
    - Only when commited during an extensive period shift priority to the project and pause other projects

This has saved me from working on something that wasn't going to work and only finding it out monhts later, and also allowed me to work on different topics in parallel. Developing an app for mobile, with music/video's requires for example licensing content, testing on different devices and typical takes longer than figuring out how to use a new back-end technology in an existing stack.

All this has now changed so drastically. During my holiday and lng drives I thought about plans and things to work on. Now, within 1 month period, I've completed almost everything the way I wanted. 

I have been able to create fully working mobile games, WebXR VR games that work on VR/Web/Mobile (in the morning my son ordered a VR game - in the afternoon he could play it in multiplayer), demo's, client facing applications.

-
Today I really feel that my restless mind, that always wondered how to achieve a goal, learn new things, is free. I have been involved in a lot of complex projects, did a lot of research on technologies and can put this all to use with AI. As long as I am able to understand what I want, I now can simply rely on getting the endresult.

My testing skills have improved, my product owner capacilities, requirements management, patience (building something is fun, waiting to get AI to make the same mistake is sometimes boring, but still is quicker).

I've have no more software ideas for now, meaning I can now finally work on reviving my collections of Robots starting with the Zenbo!
